1. Visual Configuration: Fina Interface

From version v3.6.0 onwards, Fina prioritizes **visual configuration** from within the app itself.

📍 How to configure?
Open Fina and go to:
Settings > Intelligence Domain > Services & APIs
Fill the forms with your API keys and click "Save".

2. Voice Models

A. Listening: Vosk

Download models from Vosk and place them in ~/.config/Fina/model/.

B. Speaking: Piper (TTS)

Get .onnx files from Piper and save them in ~/.config/Fina/voice_models/.

3. Smart Home (IoT)

🏠 New: Habitat Panel (V3.6.0)

From Settings > Habitat you can now configure the IPs for your newly supported devices:

  • Climate: Full control and monthly energy reading (kWh) of Air Conditioners.
  • Blinds & Windows: Bases for motorized blinds and garage doors (e.g. Somfy).
  • Cleaning Robots: Control & telemetry for Roomba/Roborock.
  • Smart Appliances: Read freezer/fridge temperatures and diagnostics.
  • Irrigation: Direct link to your smart sprinklers.

Pro Tip: Room Assignment
When a device is detected, click the Pencil icon to assign it to a "Room". This prevents Fina from getting confused if you have multiple TVs.
